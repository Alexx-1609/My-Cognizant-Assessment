Community News

Our neighbourhood is buzzing! This spring the CommunityHub launched 24 new events spanning food, music, and wellness. Volunteers organised the largest park cleanup in five years, gathering more than 300 residents on a single Saturday.

The town council approved funding for two new public stages, and a brand-new farmer's market opens every Sunday by the river. Local artists will showcase murals along Main Street throughout the summer.
