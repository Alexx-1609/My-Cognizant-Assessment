Help & Support

Guides for using the Community Event Portal.

Browsing Events

Use the filters on the Events section to narrow by category or location. Live search updates instantly as you type.

Registering

Click the "Register" button on any event card or use the full Registration form for detailed info. You'll receive a confirmation popup.

Saving Preferences

Your preferred event type is saved locally so it's pre-selected next visit. Use "Clear Preferences" to wipe it.

Finding Nearby Events

Click "Find Nearby" on the hero. We'll request your location (with your permission) and recommend the closest event.
